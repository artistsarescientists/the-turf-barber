COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be home when Zaiyden cuts my lawn?

No! As long as backyard gates are unlocked and dogs are secured inside, Zaiyden can complete your cut. He will text you before arrival and send a completion photo when done.

How do I pay?

Payment is made after your cut is completed. We accept Venmo, Zelle, or cash left in an envelope under your doormat. Simple and hassle-free.

What happens if it rains?

Electric mowing should never be done on waterlogged turf for safety and lawn health. In the event of Texas storms, Zaiyden will text you to reschedule for the next clear evening slot.

Do you bag the grass clippings?

Yes! Unlike cheap blow-and-go crews that leave clumps on your sidewalk or blow clippings into the storm drain, The Turf Barber bags and removes all clippings as part of the price.
